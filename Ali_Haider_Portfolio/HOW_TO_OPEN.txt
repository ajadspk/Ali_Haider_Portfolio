ALI HAIDER PORTFOLIO

HOW TO OPEN
1. Right-click this ZIP file and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted Ali_Haider_Portfolio folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open the website in Chrome or Edge.

Keep styles.css, script.js, projects.json, and the assets folder together with index.html.
An internet connection is needed for the online Google Fonts used by the design.
